Good food moves in three steps.

01

Share the surplus

Canteen staff add a food listing with portions, pickup location, and a clear collection deadline.

02

Find what you need

Students and campus community members browse available food and check the pickup details.

03

Reserve. Collect. Repeat.

Reserve a listing, collect it on time, and help make better use of food already prepared.
